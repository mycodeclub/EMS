# Competitor review: Tankhwa Patra vs EMS

*Prepared 4 October 2026 from Tankhwa Patra's public website and review listings.*

## 1. What Tankhwa Patra offers

### Plans (per user, per year)

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Dreamer** | ₹600 | Employee management, attendance, shifts, leave, payroll, PF/ESIC, overtime, loans and advances, mobile app (ESS) |
| **Performer** | ₹960 | Dreamer + pre-boarding, onboarding, offboarding, visit management, expense management, 1,500+ HR templates |
| **Achiever** | ₹1,320 | Performer + task management, performance management (goals, OKRs, 360° feedback, appraisal cycles), asset management, helpdesk |

**Add-on:** GPS tracking for field employees at **₹100 per user per month** (₹1,200 a year), which costs more than any plan.

### Other things they sell or claim

- Their own face-recognition attendance devices: **TP Face ₹14,499**, **TP Face Pro ₹14,999**, **TP Face 4G ₹24,599**.
- "Mirayo" AI chatbot for HR questions, visitor gate passes, and geo-fenced punch-in to prevent proxy attendance.
- "Trusted by 1,000+ organisations, 75K+ active users", naming clients such as Tata Motors and Bajaj. Rated 4.6/5 on Capterra (13 reviews).
- Their site says "Start for FREE Trial", but Capterra lists no free trial. Pricing is published only on their own site.

## 2. Where EMS is already stronger

| Area | EMS |
|---|---|
| **Try before you buy** | A live demo with four one-click role logins (Admin, HR, Accounts, Employee), reset every midnight. No sales call needed. Tankhwa Patra only offers "Book a demo". |
| **24x7 and night shifts** | A night shift counts on the day it starts. Grace time, late, half-day and full-day rules come from each shift. Suited to hospitals, hotels and schools. |
| **Works with any biometric device** | Imports the export file from any device, checking every row before saving. Device IDs are kept per employee over time, so an ID can be reused safely. Tankhwa Patra pushes its own hardware. |
| **Letters from real data** | Offer, appraisal and relieving letters are generated from the employee's record, with a full resignation and notice-period workflow. |
| **Data safety** | Records are never permanently deleted, so there is always an audit trail. |
| **Customer support** | The super admin can open any customer's panel in one click, in a separate session. |

## 3. What EMS is missing, in order of importance

| Priority | Gap | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Must have** | **Statutory payroll:** salary breakdown (basic, HRA, allowances), PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS, labour welfare fund, government upload files (ECR, challans), Form 16 | Their cheapest plan includes PF/ESIC. Without it, EMS is not yet a full payroll product. |
| **Must have** | **Phone app** (an installable web app is enough at first) with **selfie and location punch-in** | Their mobile app is a core selling point. |
| **Must have** | **Live sync from biometric devices:** eSSL and ZKTeco devices sending punches straight to the cloud | Removes the manual file upload. |
| **Must have** | Overtime, loans and advances, bank salary transfer file, Tally export | All included in their cheapest plan. |
| **Should have** | Leave policy and holiday calendar settings, reports and exports, WhatsApp payslips and alerts, multi-branch management | Day-to-day needs of growing customers. |
| **Should have** | Digital onboarding (document upload, offer acceptance, joining checklist), expense claims | Matches their middle plan. |
| **Later** | Performance reviews, asset management, helpdesk, tasks, visitor pass | Their top plan; less important for our target customers. |

## 4. Proposed offer to beat them

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | ₹0, up to 10 employees | Attendance, shifts, leave, employee self-service, import from any device |
| **Standard** | **₹480 per user per year** (₹40 a month) | Everything in Free, plus statutory payroll, overtime, loans, letters and the phone app. **20% cheaper than their ₹600 Dreamer plan.** |
| **Pro** | **₹900 per user per year** (₹75 a month) | Everything in Standard, plus **selfie and location attendance at no extra cost** (they charge ₹1,200 a year extra), onboarding, expenses, WhatsApp alerts and multi-branch |

### Positioning

> **Built for 24x7 workplaces. Works with the biometric machine you already own.**
> Try the live demo now, no sales call. GPS attendance included, not ₹100 extra.

An AI HR assistant can follow later as a further differentiator.

## 5. Suggested build order

1. **Statutory payroll:** salary structure, PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS, labour welfare fund, government files, Form 16.
2. **Phone app** with selfie and location punch-in.
3. **Live biometric device sync** (eSSL / ZKTeco).
4. Overtime, loans and advances, bank transfer file, Tally export.
5. Leave policy and holiday settings, reports, WhatsApp notifications, multi-branch.

The first three close most of the gap with their entry plan.
